Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Novaspot ("we", "us") handles personal data in the RoomFind app ("RoomFind", "the Service"). By using RoomFind you agree to this policy.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: email address, and basic profile details if you sign in with Google.
  • Profile data you add: username, bio, profile photo, WhatsApp number, contact email.
  • Listing data you add: dormitory and room names, descriptions, photos, address/location text, rent, caution deposit, availability.
  • Technical data: basic logs and device/network information generated by our hosting providers for security and operation.

We do not collect precise device location, payment details, or contacts. We do not run third-party advertising or analytics in the app.

2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in (including one-time codes sent by email).
  • To display your profile and listings to other users, and let people contact you.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Service, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. What is public

Your username, bio, profile photo, listings, photos, and the contact details (WhatsApp number and email) you put on your profile or listings are visible to other users of RoomFind. Only add contact details you are happy to share.

4. Service providers

We use Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage, and Google for optional "Continue with Google" sign-in. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can edit your profile at any time and delete your account in the app (Account → Delete Account); this removes your account, profile, and listings. Backups and logs may persist for a limited period before being purged.

6. Security

We use access controls and encrypted connections, but no system is completely secure. Keep your login email account secure.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may request access, correction, or erasure of your data, or withdraw consent, by contacting us below.

8. Children

RoomFind is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children; contact us if you believe a child has provided data and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Continued use means you accept the update.
